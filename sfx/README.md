# sfx/

Recorded sound effects used by the site. Most sounds are synthesised in the browser; these are the exceptions.

| File | What | Source and licence |
|---|---|---|
| `ah.ogg` | The satisfied "ahh" after he drinks, in "The pour" (scene 1) | Cut from "Sip of coffee, swallow then \"ah\"" by odditonic, https://freesound.org/people/odditonic/sounds/194808/ , released under Creative Commons 0 (public domain), so no credit is required. We credit them anyway. We used the site's preview file, trimmed to the "ah" with short fades and made mono. |

If the file is missing or blocked, the site plays a synthesised "ahh" instead.
